# MeshLog AI Platform - Technical Vision Brief

**Leveraging WiFi Expertise with AI to Deliver Superior Network Intelligence**

---

## The Vision

**MeshLog** transforms WNC's 15+ years of WiFi Mesh expertise into an AI-powered platform that serves dual purposes:

1. **Quality Demonstration Platform**: Makes WNC's SON (Self-Organizing Network) intelligence *visible and measurable*—proving how WNC Mesh solves real-world problems (sticky clients, backhaul bottlenecks, interference) that generic mesh systems cannot

2. **Advanced Troubleshooting Intelligence**: Embeds deep WiFi domain expertise (802.11k/v/r steering, adaptive RRM, backhaul optimization) into AI agents, democratizing expert-level analysis that currently requires rare WiFi protocol specialists

**The Opportunity**: WNC has unique WiFi Mesh SON technology that solves critical problems—MeshLog makes this superiority *provable and visible* while providing unmatched troubleshooting capabilities.

---

## The Technical Challenge

**Real-World Problem: "Strong Signal, Poor Performance"**
- Users see full WiFi bars but experience slow speeds, buffering, connectivity issues
- Root causes: Inefficient backhaul, sticky clients, poor steering, interference
- **WNC SON solves these**: Dynamic backhaul, proactive 802.11k/v/r steering, adaptive RRM, load balancing

**The Dual Challenge:**

1. **Quality Demonstration**: How do we *prove* WNC's SON intelligence is working correctly?
   - Customers can't see 802.11k/v/r steering in action
   - Backhaul optimization is invisible to users
   - Adaptive RRM channel changes happen automatically
   - Need to demonstrate competitive superiority with evidence

2. **Troubleshooting Complexity**: When issues occur, diagnosis requires deep expertise
   - WiFi protocol knowledge (802.11k/v/r, DFS, channel management)
   - Understanding mesh-specific behaviors (sticky clients, backhaul, topology)
   - Context awareness (deployment type, client mix, RF environment)
   - 2-4 hours of expert analysis per incident
